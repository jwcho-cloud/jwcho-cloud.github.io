IR Materials Explorer

Compare the refractive index n, extinction coefficient κ and absorption coefficient α of infrared coating and window materials. Set an absorption threshold to see where each material is transparent, and hover over any curve to read its values in both plots.

Details on every material are in J.-W. Cho, T. Kumar, H. Mei, and M. A. Kats, “Material selection for mid-infrared coatings and windows” (2026). arXiv:2605.29079 →
